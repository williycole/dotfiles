---
description: Speak my whole last reply, unabridged
---

Run `~/.claude/hooks/say-reply.sh --last "<scratchpad dir>/say-last.txt"`, where scratchpad dir is the session scratchpad directory named in your system prompt. If the script reports no saved reply, write your previous reply's text to that path yourself, then rerun.

Reply with one short line. Do not restate the reply text.
